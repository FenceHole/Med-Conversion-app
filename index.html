Med Conversion

Approximate dose equivalents for stimulants and mood stabilizers.

Educational reference only. Equivalents are approximate, derived from published guidance and consensus ranges, and do not account for an individual patient's pharmacokinetics, tolerance, comorbidities, or interactions. Cross-titration, serum-level monitoring, and prescriber judgment are required. Not a substitute for clinical decision-making.

Conversions use methylphenidate-IR milligram equivalents as a common unit. Amphetamine-class agents are approximately twice as potent per mg as methylphenidate. Long-acting formulations are normalized to their typical daily delivered dose.
